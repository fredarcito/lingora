Piper voices (the light voice)
==============================

Lingora downloads these voices on demand from the Piper voice catalogue
(https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices), in the format sherpa-onnx
publishes (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models).
Each voice has its own licence, which comes from the recordings it was
trained on. Only voices whose licence allows commercial use are offered.

Spanish
-------

es_ES-sharvard-medium (two speakers)
  Dataset: Sharvard corpus, University of Edinburgh
           https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/574
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
           http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

es_ES-davefx-medium
  Dataset: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)

es_MX-claude-high
  Voice:   https://huggingface.co/spaces/HirCoir/Piper-TTS-Spanish
  Licence: Apache License 2.0 (text in Apache-2.0.txt)

es_MX-ald-medium
  Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/rmcpantoja/Ald_Mexican_Spanish_speech_dataset
  Licence: The Unlicense (public domain) http://unlicense.org

es_AR-daniela-high
  Dataset: Crowdsourced high-quality Argentinian Spanish speech data set,
           Google, https://www.openslr.org/61/
  Trained by: https://huggingface.co/larcanio/piper-voices
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
